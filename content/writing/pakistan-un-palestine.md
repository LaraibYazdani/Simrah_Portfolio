Right after joining the UN in 1947, Pakistan played an active role on the issue of Palestine. Although not an Arab state, Pakistan strongly supported the creation of a single, united Palestinian state where all communities could live with equal rights, instead of dividing the land into two states.

Pakistan pushed this idea at the UN, but the proposal was rejected. When the vote on partition came up in November 1947, events took a surprising turn.

This marked the beginning of Pakistan’s long-standing commitment to the Palestinian cause on the world stage.
