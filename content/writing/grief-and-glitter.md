It’s a strange, disorienting thing to be online right now. You open your phone and the timeline shifts from a GRWM makeup video to a clip of a family’s home reduced to rubble, from someone’s dance trend to a headline about another protest, another war, another genocide. Palestine, Ukraine, Nepal, Iran, everywhere the world feels like it’s burning, and yet the scroll continues.

There’s a haunting contrast between destruction and distraction, between lives lost and lives curated for content. And somewhere in between, we’re left asking: how do we keep going, how do we bear witness, how do we not grow numb when the feed keeps mixing grief with glitter?
